Bug Sync — Privacy Policy

Last updated: April 2026

What data Bug Sync handles

When you use Bug Sync, the following data is processed:

  • Bug descriptions you type are sent to the Anthropic Claude API for ticket generation.
  • Screenshots you attach are sent to the Anthropic Claude API for analysis.
  • Generated tickets are posted to your Jira instance when you click Post to Jira.
  • Notifications are sent to your Microsoft Teams channel via webhook when configured.

What Bug Sync does NOT do

  • No data is stored on Bug Sync servers. Each session is ephemeral — when you close the tab, the data is gone.
  • No tracking, analytics, or third-party advertising.
  • No user accounts, no persistent cookies, no session identifiers.
  • Credentials (Anthropic API key, Jira token, Teams webhook URL) are stored as Vercel environment variables, never exposed to the browser.

Third-party services

Bug Sync relies on these services, each with their own privacy policies:

  • Anthropic (Claude API) — for AI ticket generation
  • Atlassian (Jira) — for ticket posting
  • Microsoft (Teams) — for notifications
  • Vercel — hosting provider

Contact

For privacy questions, contact the app owner through your organization's internal channels.
